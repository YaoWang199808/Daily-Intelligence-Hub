Long distance optical frequency transfer with short-term phase noise suppressed by a clean-up-supported remote terminal

Authors: Przemysław Krehlik, Tomasz Kawalec, Jakub Dobosz, Marcin Bober, Krzysztof Turza, Łukasz Śliwczyński, Łukasz Buczek, Antoni Weglarz, Adam M. Wojciechowski, Mariusz Semczuk

Institution: AGH University of Krakow, Jagiellonian University, University of Warsaw, Nicolaus Copernicus University, Poznan Supercomputing and Networking Center

Published: 2026-11-01

Journal: Measurement

Method: Experimental

Citations: 0

Abstract:

We demonstrate long-distance optical frequency transfer with improved short-term stability using a clean-up-supported remote terminal. Standard fiber links with active phase noise cancellation achieve excellent long-term performance, but leave residual noise at short timescales which makes the real-time use difficult. To address this problem, we implemented a transportable clean-up system at the remote end of a 650 km stabilized fiber link. The system, based on a laser locked to a commercial ultra-stable cavity, effectively suppresses the residual noise and restores a narrow-line optical reference suitable for direct applications. We show that such terminals enable reliable frequency dissemination to remote laboratories and are well suited for future national and international networks.
