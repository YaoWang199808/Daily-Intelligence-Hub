Data-driven control for orbit transition of piezoelectric energy harvesters under stochastic excitation

Authors: Jiani Xu, Hang Zhang, Xianbin Liu, Yang Li

Institution: Nanjing University of Science and Technology, Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics

Published: 2026-10-11

Journal: Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing

Method: Numerical Simulation

Citations: 0

Abstract:

To address the tendency of bistable piezoelectric energy harvesting systems to remain trapped in low-energy intrawell oscillations and the difficulty of sustaining high-energy interwell motion under stochastic disturbances, this study proposes a data-driven event-triggered jump–drift hybrid control method. The proposed method takes the neighborhood of a high-energy reference orbit as the control target and constructs a complete electromechanical state representation using displacement, velocity, and piezoelectric voltage. A signed orbit error is further defined through local radial projection to characterize both the magnitude and direction of the state deviation, thereby enabling direction-selective event triggering and control release. During the active control phase, a locally corrective control input is generated using an offline-identified one-step prediction model. Once the high-energy state is established, the controller switches to the drift mode and intervenes again only when a pronounced degradation of the system state is detected. Numerical results demonstrate that the proposed method can drive the system from a low-energy state toward the high-energy orbit while achieving a favorable trade-off among orbit-transition speed, maintenance of high-energy motion, and control effort. The method also retains a certain degree of adaptability under stochastic disturbances and mismatches in load resistance and mechanical damping. Further analyses of the sampling period and control delay indicate that the proposed method exhibits reasonable tolerance to nonidealities associated with digital implementation.

Measurement of predefined surface defect visibility using TLS intensity visualisation

Authors: Aleksandra Mazurek, Maria Elżbieta Kowalska, Artur Piekarczuk, Janina Zaczek-Peplińska

Institution: Instytut Techniki Budowlanej, Warsaw University of Technology

Published: 2026-10-09

Journal: Measurement

Method: Experimental

Citations: 0

Abstract:

Reliable detection of surface defects is essential for assessing the condition of engineering structures. Although terrestrial laser scanning (TLS) is increasingly used as a non-contact inspection technique, the diagnostic potential of TLS intensity data remains insufficiently exploited because recorded intensity depends on acquisition conditions, scanner characteristics and data visualisation. This study proposes a structured methodology for the qualitative assessment of predefined surface defect visibility in TLS intensity visualisations. The analysis included two TLS scanners, three acquisition distances (5, 10 and 15 m), sixteen intensity visualisation scales and twenty-eight predefined Regions of Interest (ROIs). A reference observer evaluated each ROI using a binary positive/not-positive identification criterion under controlled observation conditions. The Positive ROI Identification Rate (PIR) was used as the primary descriptive measure, while statistical inference was performed using a generalised linear mixed-effects model (GLMM) accounting for repeated evaluations of the same physical defects. Several continuous colour scales achieved the highest average PIR values (approximately 70–76%). The variability between measurement configurations was substantially greater than between the best-performing visualisation scales. The GLMM demonstrated significant effects of visualisation scale and the scanner × acquisition distance interaction, whereas ROI class was not statistically significant. The results indicate that positive ROI identification depends primarily on acquisition conditions, scanner characteristics, and visualisation, whereas ROI class did not significantly influence the results. The proposed methodology provides a statistically evaluated framework for comparing predefined surface defect visibility in TLS intensity data under controlled observation conditions.
