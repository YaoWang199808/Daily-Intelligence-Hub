Nonlinear vibrations of a marine propulsion shafting rotor system considering bolt loosening and interface friction coupling

Authors: Xuefei Zhang, Yuefang Wang, Bin Niu

Institution: Dalian University of Technology

Published: 2026-10-18

Journal: Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing

Method: Hybrid

Citations: 0

Abstract:

As a critical component of marine propulsion shafting, bolted joints are prone to loosening under the influence of alternating loads and vibration impacts, leading to reduced connection stiffness and potentially inducing thrust bearing misalignment as well as mechanical-magnetic coupling faults. Moreover, under low-speed and heavy-load conditions, bolt loosening exacerbates interface friction in the stern bearing, severely compromising the safe operation of the power system. In response to the issues, a dynamic modeling method for bolt loosening based on the equivalent crack theory is proposed. This method accurately captures the periodic opening and closing behavior of the connection interface during bolt loosening through the breathing effect of the crack. Furthermore, by comprehensively considering the frictional excitation of the stern bearing and the misalignment effect of the permanent magnet thrust bearing, a coupled shafting rotor dynamic model integrating friction and bolt loosening faults is established. Through numerical simulation and experimental validation, the effects of bolt loosening, thrust bearing misalignment, and stern bearing stiffness on the nonlinear vibration response of the shafting are investigated. The vibration response characteristics and evolution laws of bolt loosening faults are characterized, and the dominant excitation sources governing the vibration response at different fault stages are identified, providing support for bolt loosening state recognition based on vibration characteristics.

Sound field shaping in thermoacoustic cells via a synergistic hybrid tuning framework: Symmetry breaking, active excitation and impedance tuning

Authors: Fanhao Guo, Jingtao Du, Yan Yu, Yang Liu

Institution: Harbin Engineering University

Published: 2026-10-18

Journal: Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing

Method: Hybrid

Citations: 0

Abstract:

Sound field shaping, particularly in the contexts of acoustic focusing and cloaking, remains one of the most challenging tasks in modern wave manipulation, necessitating precise control over energy distribution and propagation paths. However, conventional passive methods frequently have fundamental limitations in tunability and dynamic response, resulting in inefficient manipulation under complex boundary conditions. To address these issues, this paper proposes a synergistic hybrid tuning framework for thermoacoustic cells that achieves a sound-pressure-level control range of Δ L p = 189 dB by jointly combining temperature-gradient-driven symmetry breaking, active acoustic excitation, and general impedance boundary tuning. Each mechanism, applied in isolation, is inherently bounded: symmetry breaking cannot inject energy, active excitation cannot reconfigure the modal landscape, and impedance tuning operates within a passive design space. Their coordinated integration, however, yields a control authority that far exceeds the sum of their individual contributions. To realize this framework computationally, a spectral improved-Fourier-Galerkin method is developed for solving the linear thermoacoustic governing equations under full thermo-viscous coupling, enabling efficient free-modal and forced-response analyses with general impedance boundary conditions. A preliminary experimental validation, carried out on a thermoacoustic cell with one rigid and one open termination, confirms the predicted onset frequencies to within 1.6 %. Free-modal analysis reveals that the temperature gradient shifts the first eigenfrequency upward by 18.8 % and induces topological twists in eigenvector trajectories, thereby breaking the spatiotemporal separability of the sound field. In forced response, the boundary auxiliary function autonomously adapts its spectral weights to boundary impedance variations, confirming its capacity for dynamic boundary reconfiguration without reformulating basis functions. A parametric study across the three control degrees of freedom quantitatively demonstrates that only their synergistic coordination reaches the full 189 dB control range; any subset of mechanisms yields substantially narrower dynamic ranges. This control range is a theoretical upper bound obtained under linear conditions. The framework provides a theoretical foundation for high-authority sound field control in thermoacoustic systems, with direct implications for the design of adaptive thermoacoustic actuators and controllable acoustic waveguides.

A multi-scale progressive fusion method for predicting the remaining useful life of gears guided by a control-equation-compatible a priori cascade

Authors: Yuliang Chen, Jing Wei, Baoping Tang, Haibo Wei, Yan Li, Siyu Chen

Institution: Chongqing University of Technology, Chongqing University, State Key Laboratory of Mechanical Transmission

Published: 2026-10-18

Journal: Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing

Method: Other

Citations: 0

Abstract:

Gears are core transmission components of high-end equipment in aerospace, rail transit and other key fields. Accurate remaining useful life (RUL) prediction of gears is critical for equipment prognostics and health management (PHM), to ensure operational safety and reduce maintenance costs. Aiming at key limitations of existing data-driven gear RUL prediction methods, including strong data dependency, insufficient physical interpretability, low mapping accuracy and weak cross-condition generalization, this paper proposes a novel progressive multi-level feature fusion RUL prediction framework guided by cascaded control equation compatibility priors. Taking gear vibration time-domain signals as the sole input, the framework sequentially adopts three customized modules: Governing Equations Auto-Encoder (GEAE) to extract physically consistent robust degradation features, Multi-scale Fusion Network (MsNet) to construct health indicators for degradation characterization, and Dual Temporal Constraints Network (DTCNet) to achieve accurate RUL mapping. Systematic validation on two heterogeneous datasets demonstrates that the framework reduces RMSE by up to 40.6% and improves R 2 by 25.0% over end-to-end methods, while significantly enhancing HI monotonicity and trendability. Cross-seed statistical tests confirm its robustness (coefficient of variation ≤ 1.9%). The method consistently outperforms state-of-the-art approaches and exhibits strong cross-scenario generalization.
