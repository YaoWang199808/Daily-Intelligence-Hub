Beyond cycle-aware segmentation: Spectral–temporal relational learning for irregular battery RUL prediction

Authors: Conghui Li, Yixuan Chen, Chern Hong Lim

Institution: Monash University Malaysia

Published: 2026-11-21

Journal: Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing

Method: Hybrid

Citations: 0

Abstract:

Remaining useful life (RUL) prediction for lithium-ion batteries is difficult when the available measurements are irregularly sampled, organized by operating cycles, and coupled across sensor variables in ways that change during degradation. Many irregular time-series methods rely on fixed-window patching and mainly model temporal correlations, which can split charge–discharge cycles and miss frequency-domain information. This paper proposes the Cycle-Aware Spectral–Temporal Graph Neural Network (CAST-GNN), which uses each operational cycle as a dual-domain modeling unit for battery prognostics. Raw multivariate records are first partitioned into cycle-aware patches using consecutive discharge-onset events. For each sensor variable within a cycle, CAST-GNN builds a raw irregular temporal view and a resampled spectral view. The two views are encoded by a Transformable Time-aware Convolution Network and by a fast Fourier transform followed by a multilayer perceptron (FFT–MLP), respectively. The resulting variable-wise embeddings are contextualized across cycles by a Transformer. Inter-variable dependencies are then modeled through a Dynamic Time-Aware Graph and a smoothed Spectral Coherence Graph, fused by a cycle-conditioned gate, and aggregated by hierarchical attention pooling for battery-level RUL regression. Experiments on the NASA, CALCE, and UNIBO benchmarks show lower prediction errors than representative baselines, and an additional experiment on commercial cells illustrates the model behavior under heterogeneous operating conditions.

Shear modulus and Poisson’s ratio master curves of viscoelastic materials using combined longitudinal ultrasonic and shear rheometry measurements: application to bitumen

Authors: Didier Laux, Rania Laghrissi, Hanaa Houjeij, Céline Monsanglant, Faoulat Miradji, Benjamin Burie, Jean-Yves Ferrandis

Institution: Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique, Université de Montpellier, Institut d'Électronique et des Systèmes, Autorité de sûreté nucléaire et de radioprotection, Institut de Radioprotection et de Sûreté Nucléaire

Published: 2026-11-01

Journal: Ultrasonics

Method: Experimental

Citations: 0

Abstract:

We propose a methodology for the ultra-wideband characterisation of highly attenuating viscoelastic materials based on ultrasonic longitudinal-wave transmission measurements and shear rheometry performed over a range of temperatures, together with the application of the Time-Temperature Superposition Principle to both rheological and ultrasonic datasets. The approach is demonstrated on a 7 0 / 1 0 0 penetration grade bitumen, for which the constitutive behaviour is identified over a wide frequency spectrum. A rheological model is shown to accurately represent both the complex shear modulus and the complex Poisson’s ratio. Unlike conventional approaches, the proposed methodology does not require Dynamic Mechanical Analysis to determine the complex Young’s modulus. It is therefore well suited for the characterisation of bituminous binders, mastics, and more generally viscoelastic materials exhibiting complex rheological behaviour, especially in situations where shear-wave ultrasonic measurements are challenging because of excessive attenuation.
