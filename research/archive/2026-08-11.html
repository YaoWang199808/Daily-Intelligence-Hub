Dynamic response model of machine tool scanning probe at high scanning speeds

Authors: Katarzyna Matys-Popielska, J.R.R. Mayer, Adam Woźniak, Rachid Guiassa

Institution: Warsaw University of Technology, Polytechnique Montréal, Cégep Marie-Victorin

Published: 2026-08-01

Journal: Measurement

Method: Experimental

Citations: 0

Abstract:

Improving quality and decreasing production time are essential factors in industrial development. For this purpose, touch-trigger probes are mounted on machine tools to enable measurement of the workpiece, intermittently with the process or on the finished part. To further reduce measurement time and increase point density, scanning probes are now available. However, the use of scanning probes that measure almost continuously without removing the tip from the measured surface may result in dynamic phenomena affecting measurements. A model describing the dynamic behavior of a scanning probe based on two degrees of freedom is proposed. The model is based on the radial deflection of the stylus from its resting position, unlike the typical use of a Cartesian coordinate system. Experimental data were collected using a Renishaw OSP60 scanning probe on a five-axis machine tool by measuring three ring gauges. Components originating from the setup eccentricity, surface, and probe dynamics were distinguished. The model explains over 94% of the dynamic behavior with a mean absolute error of less than 1.5 μm.
