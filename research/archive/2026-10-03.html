An inerter-based lumped-parameter model using improved legendre polynomials for time-domain analysis of soil-structure interaction

Authors: Jue Wang, Chaofan Ren, Yinuo Wang, Yongxin Wu

Institution: Xi'an Polytechnic University, Hohai University

Published: 2026-10-02

Journal: Engineering Structures

Method: Hybrid

Citations: 0

Abstract:

The foundation impedances describing dynamic soil-structure interaction (SSI) are frequency-dependent, rendering the utilization of impedance challenging for time-domain analysis of structural dynamic responses. A lumped-parameter model (LPM) composed of frequency-independent spring, damper, and inerter is proposed to represent the frequency-dependent characteristics of dynamic impedance, thereby facilitating direct time-domain seismic analysis of structures under SSI effects. On the basis of the orthogonality of the improved complex Legendre polynomials, the dynamic impedance of the foundation is approximated through the least-squares curve fitting technique. Subsequent partial fraction decomposition of these polynomial expressions allows their equivalent representation as assemblies of first and second-order discrete mechanical elements, which are used to identify the coefficients of the mechanical components in the LPM. Numerical examples indicate that the proposed model achieves a more accurate representation of the frequency-dependent foundation impedance with fewer components, by employing improved Legendre polynomials rather than ordinary power polynomials. Additionally, because the proposed LPM is formulated without mass elements, it avoids the fundamental flaw where spurious inertial forces arising from base motion distort the true seismic input. Finally, the accuracy of the proposed LPM is validated through seismic response comparisons with benchmark SSI models, followed by its further application to the seismic response analysis of nonlinear superstructures.

Investigation on tensile resistance of stainless-clad steel bolted connections under single shear: Test, modelling and design

Authors: Jiachen Guo, Xiaowei Ma, Peng Chen, Lele Sun, Hai-Ting Li, Fei Xu, Jun-Zhi Liu

Institution: University of Hong Kong, Beijing Normal University, Zhuhai, Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Harbin Engineering University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Chongqing University

Published: 2026-10-02

Journal: Engineering Structures

Method: Hybrid

Citations: 0

Abstract:

This paper studies the net-section tensile behaviour of double-sided stainless-clad steel (DSSCS) bolted connections subjected to single shear. The DSSCS composite comprises 304 austenitic stainless-steel cladding layers on both sides and a Q355 low-alloy structural steel substrate in the middle. Both experimental tests and finite element analysis (FEA) were conducted to characterise the failure modes and the load-deflection responses of DSSCS bolted connections. Results demonstrated that all specimens failed by net-section fracture together with the steel plate out-of-plane deformation. Apart from several connections with a relatively large edge distance, the other connections could successfully attain their theoretical tensile resistances, without interfacial delamination observed between the cladding and substrate throughout the test process. On the basis of FEA, tensile stress distributions across the critical net section and along the steel plate thickness were visualised and compared to reproduce the development of stress redistribution and assess the effect on the ultimate tensile resistance. Subsequently, a comprehensive parametric study was carried out to study the influence of the connection edge distance, gauge distance, and the pitch distance on the net-section tensile behaviour. Finally, based on the dataset acquired from experimental tests and FEA, the applicability of current design specifications was assessed. The findings confirm that the formulations codified in EN 1993–1–1: 2022 and AISC 360–22 can provide accurate tensile capacity predictions for DSSCS bolted connections under single shear.
