Optimization of the acceptance criteria for instruments associated with the dynamic LPG measurement system

Authors: GUSTAVO PESSANHA ALVIM, Paula Fernandes de Aguiar, Florian Pradelle, Elcio Cruz de Oliveira

Institution: Pontifícia Universidade Católica do Rio de Janeiro

Published: 2026-08-01

Journal: Measurement

Method: Hybrid

Citations: 0

Abstract:

The Brazilian Institute for Metrology, Quality and Technology established a maximum expanded uncertainty of 1.2 % for the final mass of Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG), measured by dynamic systems, and univariately defines maximum errors for static pressure, temperature, and density. This study introduces a multivariate optimization approach for acceptance criteria, using static pressure, temperature, and density as variables in the calibration of seven LPG measurement systems. The application of Box-Behnken (BBD) and Doehlert experimental designs (DD), connected to the Response Surface Methodology, was effective, and good mathematical models (R 2 > 0.99 in almost cases) were obtained. The BBD and DD yielded similar results, showing only minor differences in their modeling, except when the density uncertainty is ten-times higher, when the choice of the design of experiments leads to distinct mathematical models. The analysis revealed that pressure was not significant at the studied metering stations (MS), whereas temperature and density exhibited a relevant influence in all cases. Additionally, the unpaired t -test results highlighted that it is not possible to obtain a general model for all MS, particularly to correctly calibrate the constant term and the linear and quadratic terms related to density. Even though, through this multivariate approach, it was concluded that it was possible to exceed the proposed limit values from 0.6 to 1.2 °C for the temperature and 2.3 to 3.0 kg m −3 for the density, and maintain the LPG mass within the regulation specification, avoiding the unnecessary disposal of instruments that initially did not meet the required parameters.

An improved proposal for assessing blasting-induced vibration verified by experimental studies

Authors: J. Duda, Tomasz Maleska, Damian Bęben, Piotr Bobra, Joanna Nowacka, Andrzej Wysoczański, Dawid Olszewski

Institution: Opole University of Technology, Military Institute of Engineer Technology

Published: 2026-08-01

Journal: Measurement

Method: Experimental

Citations: 0

Abstract:

There is currently no unified method for analysing vibrations caused by shallow explosions, such as quarry and military blasting. Therefore, the authors developed an improved proposal to analyse blasting-induced vibration. The proposed methodology unifies earlier methods for assessing human exposure to anthropogenic vibration with those applied to analyse mine tremors and induced seismicity. The novel approach is based on experimental analyses of ground vibration induced by explosions conducted at the military training area. Present test results suggest that blasting-induced vibration is similar to mining-induced vibration in dominant frequencies and, simultaneously, to railway-induced vibration in the ratio of maximum acceleration to maximum velocity. Thus, combining methods applied to mining-induced vibration with those intended for railway-induced vibration yields satisfactory results for blasting-induced vibration, which exhibits characteristics of both railway vibrations (a max to v max ratio of approximately 250–350) and mining-induced vibrations (low dominant frequencies between 3 and 10 Hz). The proposed method may be of great practical importance for assessing blasting-induced vibrations, including evaluating human exposure to vibration in terms of perception-level contravention.
