Evaluating health indicators on few run-to-failure units: Unit sensitivity and acquisition confounders in four public archives

Authors: Edson José Pacheco, César Augusto Dartora

Institution: Universidade Federal do Paraná

Published: 2026-11-01

Journal: Measurement

Method: Hybrid

Citations: 0

Abstract:

Raw-signal health indicators are validated by a pooled cross-validated coefficient against a reference measurement, often on few physical units. We measure how such a coefficient depends on its units by deleting one unit at a time on four public run-to-failure archives: Li-ion batteries, bearings, milling tools and fatigued lap joints. One deletion moves the pooled coefficient of determination by 0.20 to 3.14 and changes its sign in three domains, while the sign of the mean per-unit correlation survives every deletion in 30 of 40 distinct tests. The unit count also sets a floor, since a lone unanimous cell needs ten units to reach q < 0.05 after false-discovery correction over 40 tests. Four confounders are then isolated under an unchanged grouped protocol. Record duration alone reaches R 2 = + 0.478 ± 0.146 on battery capacity; a bearing reference from a channel the descriptors never see lowers that domain’s error by 44%; a per-unit reference frame multiplies the milling coefficient by 2.9; one corrupt record in 137 carries 55% of the uncorrected milling result. Two mechanisms reproduce when induced where they do not occur. The corrections also add evidence: a battery model that names discharge duration reaches R 2 = + 0.754 ± 0.115 and keeps its sign under every deletion. Battery subsets show that unit count alone can leave the sign unresolved, and in simulation the units needed vary with between-unit heterogeneity. On these archives a coefficient belongs in print beside its deletion range, read as a sensitivity, not an uncertainty.
